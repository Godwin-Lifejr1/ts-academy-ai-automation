# Module 1 — Topic 7: Examples & Practice Questions

## Example 1 — Simple automation
Job application form → Google Sheets → confirmation email → HR notification.

AI is not required because the process follows predefined instructions.

## Example 2 — AI + automation
Customer message:
"I paid for my order three days ago but nobody has delivered it. I want my money back."

AI can interpret the message and produce structured data:
intent = refund_request
issue = delivery_delay
priority = high

Automation can then validate, route, create a support ticket, and notify the relevant team.

## Example 3 — Platform selection
- Simple app connection → Zapier may be appropriate.
- Complex visual branching/data manipulation → Make may be appropriate.
- APIs, webhooks, custom code and AI → n8n may be appropriate.
- Microsoft 365-heavy organisation → Power Automate may be appropriate.
- Desktop/legacy enterprise processes → UiPath may be appropriate.

## Module 1 conclusion

Important principle:
Do not use AI merely because it is available. Use AI when the task requires capabilities that predefined rules cannot reliably provide.
